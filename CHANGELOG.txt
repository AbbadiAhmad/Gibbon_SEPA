CHANGELOG
=========

v0.0.00
-------
creating the initial module

v0.1.00
-------
Enable the searching in Families and payments views.
Move the data handling into SEPAGateway.
Add SEPA Archive ID as defaults field when installing the SEPA module. (to match the paper versions with Gibbon forms.)
Add academicYear to Payments.
Change the column name from user into gibbonUser in gibbonSEPAPaymentEntry table (not to use sql keyword).
Add familyID to payment_entry when import the payments from xls file.

v2.3.0
------
IBAN is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM, key in .env / environment) instead of masked; BIC is stored again.
Add withdrawal cadence (monthly, half-yearly, yearly) and preferred withdrawal day (default monthly, day 5) to the SEPA data, forms, views, imports and snapshot export.
Add the Family Due in Period page (balance plus the fees due in a selectable period).
Add the import of SEPA data from the registration forms (field mapping, review/edit, validation).
Remove test_masking.php.
Add Export to Excel of the family balance (with the due-in-period column on the Family Due page).
Family Due: calculate the withdraw amount (amount due in the period, capped at the open balance) and export it with IBAN, BIC, mandate date and cadence; add a withdrawals-only export.
v2.3.1: the import from registration forms is its own action in the Entry menu.
v2.3.2: Check SEPA Data page; the withdrawal export also lists families without SEPA / IBAN with a Check column.
v2.4.0: View SEPA - my family: families see their SEPA data (half hidden IBAN) and its problems; payment lists show the IBAN half hidden to families.
v2.5.0: Academic year filter (default the current year, All, or a specific year) on Payment Adjustments, Courses Fee, Manage Payments and Child Enrollment Details; Child Enrollment Details marks rows whose end date is before the start date in red; new Merge Families page (side by side comparison, choose the data to keep, merge users, move the users and merge the SEPA).
v2.5.1: SEPA check finds SEPAs whose family does not exist and offers 'Merge with other SEPA' (own permission 'Merge SEPA'); academic year filter on the unlinked payments.
v2.5.2: Courses Fee: copy the fees from the previous academic year (courses matched by name, only courses without a fee), empty field = fee not set; pages with balances/totals warn when a course with enrolled students has no fee.
v2.6.0: Families can enter changes of their SEPA data on 'View SEPA - my family' (new permission 'Edit SEPA - my family', not given to any role by default). The changes are recorded until approved in 'Approve SEPA Changes', where errors and warnings (e.g. invalid IBAN) are flagged. The family sees the current and the entered values and can change the entries or discard them.
v2.6.0 (log): every entry, change, discard, approval and rejection is logged (what, when, who); IBANs in the log stay encrypted.
v2.6.1: Families can also change the date of the SEPA signature, not earlier than the existing date (checked when entering and again on approval).
v2.7.0: The approval page lists only the requests; new SEPA Change Log page (filters, search, link to the family SEPA, delete of data older than two years); the family sees its history on its SEPA page, the SEPA view shows it to the administration.
v2.7.1: Approval page: the approval comes first, then the last 10 log lines with a link to the log of that SEPA; the reviewer accepts or rejects each change with a checkbox and can correct the values.
v2.7.2: Approval: rows the family did not change or left empty are not ticked by default; the date of the SEPA signature is shown correctly (empty = not changed, date field and log use the date format).
v2.7.3: Signature date for a SEPA without a date: zero dates count as no date, the reviewer's date accepts any date format, and a save that did not store the values is reported instead of shown as success.
v2.7.4: SEPA check treats a zero signature date (0000-00-00) as missing.
v2.8.0: Families without SEPA can enter a new SEPA for approval (same flow: entered data, flags, change/discard, approval with per-field accept and corrections); the SEPA is created on approval.
